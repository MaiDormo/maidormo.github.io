\projecthead{dist-patoh --- Distributed GPU Hypergraph Partitioning}{M.S. Thesis (In Progress), 2026--2027}
\begin{itemize}[nosep, after=\strut, leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=1pt, label=\color{accent}\textbullet]
    \item Developing a distributed multi-GPU hypergraph partitioner in \textbf{C++20, CUDA, and MPI} to split massive, irregular sparse graphs across GPUs while balancing compute load and minimizing cross-node communication.
    \item Implementing a multilevel pipeline --- parallel coarsening, recursive k-way partitioning, and local refinement --- to address the load-imbalance and scheduling bottlenecks of standard GPU sparse kernels.
    \item Currently validating multi-GPU scaling, memory-footprint reduction, and partition quality against established CPU and GPU baselines.
\end{itemize}
